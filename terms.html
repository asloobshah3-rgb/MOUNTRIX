These notes explain how this website works and the limits of the information presented here.

INFORMATION, NOT A TOUR OPERATOR

Using Summit Atlas

Summit Atlas provides mountain profiles and travel inspiration for general informational purposes. It does not sell, arrange or confirm tours, permits, accommodation, transport or guiding services.

Mountain access, weather, routes and safety conditions can change. Content on this site is not a substitute for current local advice, official guidance or qualified professional judgment.

LOCAL ACCOUNT AND REVIEW FEATURES

Accounts and reviews

Sign-up, login and review submissions run only in your browser. They are not connected to a server, do not provide secure online authentication, and do not send or publish information to Summit Atlas.

Account credentials are stored in this browser using a salted password hash. Sample reviews are illustrative; your own reviews are stored locally. Clearing this browser's site data removes locally saved information.

PRIVACY INFORMATION

What stays in this browser

When you create an account, your name, email address and a salted password hash are saved in this browser's local storage. The password itself is not saved. Your login session stays in this browser for the duration you choose.

Saved favorites and review submissions also stay in this browser. Account details, reviews and enquiries are not sent to a Summit Atlas server. Do not use a real or reused password here. Clearing site data removes locally saved information.

MEDIA AND SOURCES

External services

Mountain photographs and the homepage video are loaded from Wikimedia services and link to their source pages. Fonts and editorial landscape photographs may be loaded from Google Fonts and Unsplash. These services require an internet connection and have their own terms and privacy practices.

Some images and the video may not load in every browser or network environment; local illustrated fallbacks are provided where applicable.
